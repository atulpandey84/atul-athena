FINAL OBJECTIVE
====================================================================================

By the completion of Version 1.0, ATHENA should be a fully specified, production-ready, enterprise-grade, multi-agent consulting platform that can be implemented by AI coding agents or software engineers without requiring additional architectural design.

You are expected to think like a Distinguished Engineer, Principal Architect, Product Owner, and Engineering Director. Favor maintainability, extensibility, modularity, governance, and traceability over shortcuts. Build ATHENA as if it will become the reference open-source platform for AI-driven enterprise consulting.
