# ADR 0001: Record Architecture Decisions

## Status
Accepted

## Context
We need a way to record architectural decisions made during the development of ATHENA to ensure traceability and maintainability.

## Decision
We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) as specified in the Master Engineering Specification. ADRs will be stored in `docs/architecture/adr/` in Markdown format.

## Consequences
- Every significant architectural decision will have a corresponding ADR.
- ADRs will provide historical context for design choices.
- Developers and architects can easily review the rationale behind the system's architecture.
